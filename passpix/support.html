Frequently asked questions

Is my photo uploaded?

No. Everything happens on your device. We have no server and no account.

Will my photo be accepted?

PassPix checks the published rules (size, head height, eye line, background, lighting), but only the issuing office decides. Always check the official page linked in the app.

How do I print?

Open "Print sheet" and tap "Print" to use a printer near you, or "Share" to send the file to a photo kiosk or an online print service. Print on 10 × 15 cm (4 × 6 in) photo paper at 100 % scale, without "fit to page" or cropping. Cut along the marks.

Why does the app warn me before changing the background?

Some documents (for example Türkiye, the United States and the United Kingdom) forbid any digital change to the photo. The app still lets you change the background, but first shows a warning with a link to the official rules, and the check then reports the edit. For those documents the safest choice is to take the photo again in front of a plain, light wall.

I bought Pro on another device.

Open Settings → Restore purchase while signed in with the same Apple ID.

My document is missing.

Write to us; we add documents after checking their official rules. Meanwhile "Other countries · 35 × 45 mm" fits many countries.
