What PassPix does

PassPix helps you take and prepare photos that follow the published size and composition rules of the documents listed in the app. The checks are automatic estimates made on your device. They help, but no app can guarantee that an office will accept a photo: the final decision is always made by the issuing authority.

Document rules

Each document's rules are summarised from the official page linked in the app, with the date we last checked it. Rules can change without notice. Before you apply, check the official page. PassPix is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to any government.

Your responsibility

  • Use a photo that shows you as you look today, without filters or face edits.
  • The optional background change is your choice. Where a document's rules forbid editing, the app warns you and links to the official rules before changing it; a photo with an edited background may then be rejected. Local offices may also decide differently from the published rules.
  • Some offices require photos taken by a registered photographer or at the office; check the requirements that apply to you.
