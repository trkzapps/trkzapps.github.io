Face data

What face data the app uses. To guide you while you take a passport, ID or visa photo and to check the photo against the rules of the document you chose, the app uses Apple's Vision framework, which runs entirely on your device, on the live camera image and on the photo you take or import. This produces face data: the position and size of the face in the picture; the positions of facial features (eyes, nose, mouth and chin outline); the head's turn and tilt angles; simple ratios such as how open the eyes and mouth appear; the number of faces and people in the picture; and an outline of the person, used to find the top of the hair and to tell the person apart from the background. From these the app calculates measurements: head height, eye height, how centred the head is, and the brightness and sharpness of the face area.

How face data is used. Face data is used only to (a) show the positioning guide and instructions on the camera screen, (b) frame the photo to the document's size and check it against the document's rules for head size, eye position, head position, expression, lighting and background, and (c) when you turn it on, replace the background behind you with a plain colour. It is not used for any other purpose — not for advertising, analytics, tracking or profiling.

Where face data is stored. Face data is kept only in your device's temporary working memory (RAM) while the app is running. It is never written to your device's storage, never included in backups or iCloud, never logged and never uploaded.

How long face data is kept, and deletion. Results from the live camera are replaced by the next camera frame, several times per second, and are discarded when you leave the camera screen. Results for the photo you are checking are kept only while that photo is open in the app, and are deleted when you retake or choose another photo, when you close the photo screens, or when the app is closed.

Sharing. Face data is never shared with anyone. It never leaves your device and is never sent to us, to Google (AdMob), to Apple or to any other third party.

What the app does not do. The app does not use Face ID, TrueDepth depth data or ARKit face tracking. It does not recognise or identify people, does not create face templates or faceprints, and does not infer age, gender, ethnicity or emotion. It does not retouch, smooth or reshape faces.

Photos

  • Photos you take or import are analysed, framed and converted only on your device.
  • They are never sent to us, to advertisers or to any server.
  • Your photo is kept in the app's working memory while you edit it. When you choose Share, a copy is written to the app's protected temporary folder so the system can hand it to the app you pick; these copies are deleted every time the app starts. When you choose Save to Photos, the file goes to your photo library. When you choose Print, the print sheet goes through the iOS print dialog to the printer you select. The app keeps no photo history.
  • Exported files contain no location or device data (only the print resolution).

Permissions

  • Camera — to take your photo. Asked only when you open the camera and tap "Allow camera".
  • Photos (add only) — only when you choose "Save to Photos". The app cannot read your photo library; when you import a photo, the system picker gives the app only the image you choose.
  • Tracking (App Tracking Transparency) — only to show more relevant ads; you can decline and still use every feature.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

If you do not have PassPix Pro, the app shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect device identifiers (the advertising identifier only if you allow tracking), coarse location derived from your IP address, and ad interaction data to serve, measure and personalise ads and to prevent fraud. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent through Google's consent message, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy choices. Ads never receive your photos or face data. See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses data from partner apps. You can report an ad from the "Report this ad" link under each banner.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple; we never receive your payment details. Whether you own PassPix Pro is verified on your device through the App Store. To remember unlocks earned with videos and which exported photos you have already unlocked, the app stores a counter and one-way SHA-256 fingerprints of up to 50 exported files in your device's Keychain. A fingerprint cannot be turned back into a photo and contains no face data; it stays on your device.

Analytics

PassPix contains no analytics or crash-reporting SDK. Apple may share crash reports with us if you agreed to share them with app developers in iOS Settings.

Children

The app is not directed at children. A parent may use it to take a child's document photo; the photo and its face data stay on the parent's device and are handled exactly as described above.

Data retention and your rights

We hold no personal data on servers. Advertising data is retained by Google according to its policies. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, delete or object to processing; contact us, or Google for data held by Google. Deleting the app removes its data from your device, except the Keychain entries described under "Purchases", which iOS keeps until the device is erased.
