Face data

What face data the app uses. To guide you while you take your DV photo and to check the photo against the DV photo rules, the app uses Apple's Vision framework, which runs entirely on your device, on the live camera image and on the photo you take or import. This produces face data: the position and size of the face in the picture; the positions of facial features (eyes, nose, mouth and chin outline); the head's turn and tilt angles; simple ratios such as how open the eyes appear; the number of faces and people in the picture; and an outline of the person, used to find the top of the hair and to tell the person apart from the background. From these the app calculates measurements: head height, eye height, how centred the head is, and the brightness and sharpness of the face area.

How face data is used. Face data is used only to show the positioning guide and instructions on the camera screen, and to check and explain whether your photo meets the DV requirements for head size, eye position, head position and lighting. It is not used for any other purpose — not for advertising, analytics, tracking or profiling.

Where face data is stored. Face data is kept only in your device's temporary working memory (RAM) while the app is running. It is never written to your device's storage, never included in backups or iCloud, never logged and never uploaded.

How long face data is kept, and deletion. Results from the live camera are replaced by the next camera frame, several times per second, and are discarded when you leave the camera screen. Results for the photo you are checking are kept only while that photo is open in the app. They are deleted automatically when another photo replaces it in the app (a new photo you use or import), when you tap "Delete this photo", or when the app is closed (quit from the app switcher, or ended by iOS). Nothing about your face remains after that.

Sharing. Face data is never shared with anyone. It never leaves your device and is never sent to us, to Google (AdMob or Firebase), to Apple or to any other third party.

What the app does not do. The app does not use Face ID, the TrueDepth depth data or ARKit face tracking. It does not recognise or identify people, does not create face templates or faceprints, and does not infer age, gender, ethnicity or emotion. Passport scans are not analysed for faces.

Photos and passport scans

  • Photos you take or import, and passport pages you scan, are analysed and converted only on your device using Apple's on-device frameworks.
  • They are never sent to us, to advertisers, to analytics providers or to any server.
  • Your photo is kept in the app's working memory. A copy is written to the app's protected temporary folder only when you export it, so that you can share or save it where you choose. That copy is deleted when you export again, when you delete the photo, and every time the app starts. The app keeps no photo history.
  • Passport pages are kept in working memory only while the Passport screen is open; exported passport files are deleted from the app right after you share or save them.
  • Exported files contain no location data.

Permissions

  • Camera — to take your DV photo and scan passport pages. Asked only when you choose to turn on the camera.
  • Photos (add only) — only when you choose "Save to Photos". The app cannot read your photo library; when you import a photo, the system picker gives the app only the image you choose.
  • Tracking (App Tracking Transparency) — only to show more relevant ads; you can decline and still use every feature.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

If you do not have Pro, the app shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect device identifiers (the advertising identifier only if you allow tracking), coarse location derived from your IP address, and ad interaction data to serve, measure and personalise ads and to prevent fraud. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent through Google's consent message, and you can change your choice at any time in the app's Settings. Ads never receive your photos, your face data or your passport scans. See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses data from partner apps.

You can report an inappropriate ad from the "Report this ad" link under each banner or in Settings.

Crash reports and usage statistics (Firebase)

Firebase receives only technical crash information and automatic usage statistics. We send nothing about your photos, your face, your passport or what you do in the app: the app sends no custom events and no user properties.

  • Firebase Crashlytics receives crash reports: technical details such as the crash trace, device model, operating system version and app version. For errors the app handles itself, only the error's type and code are sent — never messages, file names or content.
  • Google Analytics for Firebase records only the statistics it collects automatically, such as app opens and sessions, which screens of the app are opened, app version, device model, operating system version, approximate location (such as country or city) derived from the IP address, and in-app purchases. It uses a random app-instance identifier. The advertising identifier is not collected, and the data is not used for advertising.

See Firebase privacy information.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple. We do not receive your payment details. Whether you own Pro is verified on your device through the App Store. To remember earned video tokens and which exported files you have already unlocked, the app stores the token count and one-way SHA-256 fingerprints of up to 50 exported files in your device's Keychain. A fingerprint cannot be turned back into a photo and contains no face data; this information stays on your device.

Children

The app is not directed at children. A parent may use it to take a child's DV photo; the photo and its face data stay on the parent's device and are handled exactly as described above.

Data retention and your rights

We do not hold any personal data on our own servers. Face data is never retained beyond the times described in "Face data" above. Advertising and analytics data are retained by Google according to its policies. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, delete or object to processing; contact us or Google for data held by Google. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device, except the Keychain entries described under "Purchases", which iOS keeps until the device is erased.
