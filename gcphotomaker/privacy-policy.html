Privacy Policy

Green Card Photo Maker & Check · Last updated: October 30, 2026

In short: your photos and passport scans are processed only on your device. We never upload them, and we have no servers that receive them. The app shows ads (unless you buy Pro) and sends anonymous crash reports and usage statistics.

Who we are

Günnur Yüzüak, Türkiye. Contact: trkzapps@gmail.com. This app is independent and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to the U.S. Department of State or any government.

Photos and passport scans

  • Photos you take or import, and passport pages you scan, are analysed and converted only on your device using Apple's on-device frameworks.
  • They are never sent to us, to advertisers, to analytics providers or to any server.
  • The app keeps no photo history: working copies are deleted when you delete the photo, after you export a passport scan, and every time the app starts.
  • Exported files contain no location data.
  • The app does not perform face recognition and does not infer age, ethnicity, emotion or identity.

Permissions

  • Camera — to take your DV photo and scan passport pages. Asked only when you first use these features.
  • Photos (add only) — only when you choose "Save to Photos".
  • Tracking (App Tracking Transparency) — only to show more relevant ads; you can decline and still use every feature.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

If you do not have Pro, the app shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect device identifiers (the advertising identifier only if you allow tracking), coarse location derived from your IP address, and ad interaction data to serve, measure and personalise ads and to prevent fraud. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent through Google's consent message, and you can change your choice at any time in the app's Settings. Ads never receive your photos or passport scans. See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses data from partner apps.

You can report an inappropriate ad from the "Report this ad" link under each banner or in Settings.

Crash reports and usage statistics (Firebase)

We use Firebase Crashlytics and Google Analytics for Firebase to fix crashes and understand which features are used. They receive technical data (device model, OS version, app version, crash traces) and coarse events such as "a photo was checked" or "an export was completed". They never receive your photos, passport scans, passport data or the results of measuring your face. The advertising identifier is not collected by Analytics. See Firebase privacy information.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple. We do not receive your payment details. Whether you own Pro is verified on your device through the App Store.

Children

The app is not directed at children. A parent may use it to take a child's DV photo; the photo stays on the parent's device like any other photo.

Data retention and your rights

We do not hold any personal data on our own servers. Advertising and analytics data are retained by Google according to its policies. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, delete or object to processing; contact us or Google for data held by Google. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device.

Changes

We will update this page when the app's data practices change and show the new date at the top.
