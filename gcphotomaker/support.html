Open the app → DV Guide → Settings → Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought Pro with.

Is the photo guaranteed to be accepted?

No app can guarantee that. The app checks the published DV digital-image rules and tells you what it cannot verify; the U.S. Department of State makes the final decision. This app is independent and not affiliated with any government.
